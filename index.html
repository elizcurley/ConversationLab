What makes this different
The other person gets to be a person.
Good communication doesn't guarantee agreement, forgiveness, a better relationship, or the outcome you hoped for. Sometimes the most useful skill is tolerating disappointment. Sometimes it's apologizing. Sometimes it's leaving.
Scenes are fictional, and the AI may still be too agreeable or inconsistent. Use the exercise to rehearse—not as a verdict about your relationships or a substitute for support in unsafe situations.
