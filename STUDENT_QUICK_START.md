# The Conversation Lab — Quick Start

**What it is:** A free website that creates a fictional person and a conversation for you to practice. You can work on everyday connection, saying no, listening, disagreement, self-advocacy, feedback, repair, or general communication.

1. **Choose your focus.** General Conversation / Open Session is a good place to begin, but you can choose a specific skill.
2. **Choose your scenario.** Select a relationship, situation type, and complexity, or leave them on “Surprise me.”
3. **Choose an AI chat.** Copilot, Gemini, Claude, and ChatGPT are supported. Recommended prompt length is selected for you; you can change it.
4. **Generate and copy.** Click **Generate a conversation** → **Copy complete prompt**.
5. **Practice.** Open a **new chat** on your AI platform, paste the prompt, and respond as yourself. The AI plays the other person. It will not always agree with you, and that's okay.
6. **Reflect.** Type **END CONVERSATION** to get feedback. For additional guidance, select Coaching by Request before generating; then `COACH PAUSE` and `RESUME` let you temporarily step outside the scene. You can also use `REHEARSE MOMENT` after the debrief with Portable or Expanded prompts to try an alternative.

**Remember:** Your goal isn't to convince everyone or obtain a perfect ending. It is to practice being clear, respectful, appropriately assertive, flexible, and attentive to other people. This is an educational rehearsal, not a replacement for therapy or support in an unsafe situation. Avoid sharing sensitive identifying details about real people.
